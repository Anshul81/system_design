## What You'll Learn

- ✅ Estimate QPS, storage, and bandwidth for any system
- ✅ Structure a 45-minute system design interview properly
- ✅ Reason about scaling decisions with trade-off analysis
- ✅ Distinguish between stateless and stateful services
- ✅ Identify and eliminate single points of failure

---

## Why This Phase Matters

The interviewer's core question is always: *"Can this handle 10x or 100x traffic?"* Whether you're designing a URL shortener or Netflix, the scaling discussion is where you differentiate yourself. This phase gives you the tools to estimate, scale, and structure your answer.

---

## Checkpoint Exercise

1. **Estimate the storage and QPS for Twitter** — show your math
2. **Walk through the 4-step framework** for a URL shortener in 5 minutes
3. **Explain the trade-offs** of horizontal vs vertical scaling for a database
